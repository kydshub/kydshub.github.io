About Me

Hi there! I’m Kimscey Yvan D. Sulit, aka KYDS and this portfolio is a delightful concoction of curiosity, caffeine, and a dash of “let’s see if this works.”

Currently, I’m diving into the world of web app development, mostly for fun and partly as an experiment to flex my brain muscles. Think of this portfolio as my playground—a digital sandbox where I’m testing ideas, breaking things (intentionally, of course), and learning how to fix them again.

When I’m not busy clicking away on my keyboard, you’ll probably find me deep in thought, wondering if coding errors are just the universe’s way of keeping me humble. This is a space to showcase some of my projects, try out cool design tricks, and maybe even convince you that I sort of know what I’m doing. (Spoiler: I totally do. Most of the time.)

Stay tuned for updates, as this portfolio evolves just like my coding skills—slowly but surely, with the occasional stroke of genius. Enjoy your visit!
